Keywords & Filter Rules
View active scoring keywords by severity tier, add custom include/suppress terms, and align filtering with the CMP
How filtering works: Each incoming news item is scored by matching its title and description against keyword tiers below. The highest-matching tier wins. Items scoring above a source's minSeverity threshold are dropped before reaching the dashboard. Per the CMP, the CMT activates for events causing extended business disruption, multi-site impact, or decisions beyond local incident management — the SEV 1/2 tiers reflect this. Custom include keywords boost matched items to SEV 2 (Major). Suppress keywords force items to SEV 5 — effectively removing them from the dashboard.
+ Custom Include Keywords matched items boosted to SEV 2
Add terms your team considers operationally significant that aren't in the built-in list. Examples: a specific threat actor name, a regulatory body action keyword, a Rocket-adjacent company name.
− Suppress Keywords matched items forced to SEV 5 / hidden
Add terms that consistently produce noise — things that sound relevant but aren't. Examples: "presidential debate", "stock market", "opinion poll", "earnings report".

SEV 4 — Moderate Background monitoring — usually filtered by minSeverity thresholds
Terror / Physical Security Any match forces category = Terror and pins to dashboard regardless of tier

⚠ CMP Severity Scale
Based on Rocket's Crisis Management Plan (CMP). Severity levels drive CMT activation thresholds. SEV 1–2 events require immediate CMT engagement; SEV 3 events trigger incident management; SEV 4–5 are monitored.
TierNameColorDescription
Tier 0 CMT activated. Enterprise-wide impact (I1). Existential threat, mass casualty, or total operational collapse.
Tier 1 CMT likely activated. Multi-site impact (I2). Active threat to life safety or major sustained disruption.
Tier 2 Incident management engaged. Site-level impact (I3). Significant disruption, credible threat, or confirmed breach.
Tier 3 Local response only. Departmental impact (I3/I4). Developing situation or advisory-level threat.
Tier 4 Informational. Minimal impact (I4). Routine monitoring, no action required.
Incident Impact Levels (CMP)
I1 — EnterpriseAll Rocket entities affected. CMT must convene immediately. I2 — Multi-Site2+ locations or business units impacted. CMT activation likely. I3 — Site/DeptSingle building or department affected. Incident management response. I4 — MinimalLimited operational effect. No CMT action needed.
